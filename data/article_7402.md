Why Does the Yamaha XMAX Lose Engine Oil?
Today, I rode continuously from Bangna-Trad Road, Bangkok, all the way to Yamaha Square Hatyai เขต8 — a total distance of 965 kilometers. The bike was ridden at full throttle, maintaining speeds of 150–160 km/h almost the entire way, from Rama II Road to Hat Yai, stopping only for fuel every 200 km before continuing the ride.
The motorcycle used was a completely stock Yamaha XMAX with standard Yamaha engine oil.
The result?
The engine oil level did not drop at all.
In my personal opinion, if anyone experiences engine oil loss, it may be caused by engine modifications. For stock motorcycles, it could also be due to using engine oil with incorrect specifications or improper viscosity grades.
If the motorcycle remains stock, receives regular maintenance according to Yamaha standards, and is properly serviced, the engine oil should not disappear regardless of riding style.
